A source-grounded curriculum in Vedic astrology, built on the framework of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra. Read the pages roughly in order to build from foundations to synthesis, or jump to any topic — each page is a full reference in its own right.

The path from beginner to synthesis

  1. Foundations — Grahas, Dignity, Rashis, Bhavas, Lagna: the alphabet of a chart.
  2. Refinement — Vargas, Aspects, Strength: how a placement is graded and confirmed.
  3. Advanced — Special Points, Yogas, Avasthas & Longevity, Dashas, Ashtakavarga: reading and timing.
