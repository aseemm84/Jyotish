Alongside the Parashari framework, BPHS carries a set of Jaimini-style tools: image-points (Arudhas), interventions (Argala), the significators-by-longitude (Chara Karakas), and the Karakamsa. Parashara sows them (Ch. 29–33); Jaimini later develops them.

Arudha Pada — the image of a house

A house shows a matter's reality; its Arudha shows how that matter appears — its projection into the world, its reputation and manifestation.

Computation — Count how many signs the house-lord sits from its house; from the lord, count that many signs again. The sign reached is the Arudha.

Exceptions — A pada can never fall on the house itself or the 7th from it. If the count lands on the same house, take the 10th from it instead; if on the 7th, take the 4th from the house. (If the lord sits in the 4th or 10th from its house, that occupied sign is the Arudha directly.)

Every house has a pada; the two most used are the Arudha Lagna (image of the self) and the Upapada (image of the 12th — a principal marriage indicator).

Bhava PadaWhat its Arudha shows
1 · Lagna Pada (Arudha Lagna, AL)the projected self — public image, status, how the world sees you
2 · Dhana Padathe image of wealth and family
3 · Vikrama Padathe image of courage & siblings
4 · Sukha (Matru) Padathe image of home, property & mother
5 · Mantra (Putra) Padathe image of children, learning & power
6 · Roga (Satru) Padathe image of enemies, debts & disease
7 · Dara Pada (DP)the image of spouse & partnerships
8 · Marana Padathe image of upheaval & endings
9 · Pitru Padathe image of fortune, father & dharma
10 · Karma Padathe image of career & status
11 · Labha Padathe image of gains & networks
12 · Upapada (Vyaya Pada, UL)the image of loss & letting-go — and the key pointer for marriage & spouse
Worked example. Scorpio Lagna with Mars (its lord) in Cancer — Mars is 9 signs from the Lagna; counting 9 signs on from Cancer reaches Pisces. So Pisces is the Arudha Lagna: the person's public image is coloured by Pisces even though the body/self is Scorpio.

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 29 (Bhava Padas — Arudha computation, the exceptions, and the twelve named padas incl. Lagna Pada and Dara Pada); graha-arudhas (a pada for each planet) are given in the same chapter.

Reading the Arudha Lagna & Upapada

  • Benefics in or aspecting the Arudha Lagna raise status and public regard; malefics can bring notoriety or a harsh image. Wealth is read from the 2nd from AL (the Arudha of wealth).
  • The Upapada (UL) and its lord describe the spouse and the marriage's dignity; the 2nd from UL shows the sustenance/longevity of the marriage. Malefics there can indicate strain — read cautiously and never as a fixed verdict.

Argala — intervention

Argala is a 'bolt' — a planet in certain houses from a point intervenes in its results; a planet in the opposing house obstructs that intervention (Virodhargala).

2nd12th
4th10th
11th3rd
5th9th
3rd (by malefics especially)(counter by the 11th)
  • Benefic argala helps the matters of the point; malefic argala stresses them; both give mixed results.
  • Argala on the Lagna and the Arudha Lagna (and their 7ths) is read for fame and fortune.

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 31 (Argala and its obstruction; effects of unobstructed argala on each house).

Chara Karakas — the significators by longitude

Rank the planets by degree within their sign, highest first; each rank becomes a movable significator. BPHS's eight-karaka scheme:

Atmakaraka (AK)the self, soul, the inner ruler — the most important karakahighest longitude
Amatyakaraka (AmK)career, counsel, livelihood, the mind's minister2nd highest
Bhratrikaraka (BK)siblings, guru, courage, discrimination3rd

Translator / commentary. A seven-karaka variant is also used (merging Pitri/Putra into a single Putra-karaka and dropping one planet); some schemes include Rahu, counting its longitude in reverse. State which scheme you use.

Read the karakas as living significators alongside the fixed houses: the AK is the soul's agenda (strongest planet, inner king), the AmK the career/mind, the DK the spouse.

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 32, vv. 13–17 (the chara-karaka order and status).

Karakamsa — the soul's navamsa

The Karakamsa is the Navamsa (D9) sign occupied by the Atmakaraka, projected back into the rasi as a reference lagna. Read from it, planets and signs describe the soul's deeper agenda — vocation, spirituality, and the karmic themes of the life.

  • Planets in and aspecting the Karakamsa show innate talents and drives (e.g. benefics for learning and grace, malefics for sharp or technical gifts).
  • Many Raja Yogas are judged from the Karakamsa together with the Atmakaraka and the Putrakaraka (see the Yogas page).

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 33 (Karakamsa); applied throughout the Raja Yoga chapter, Ch. 39.

Study note. Compute these once per chart and keep them beside the rasi chart. The houses answer 'what is', the Arudhas answer 'how it looks', and the Karakamsa answers 'what the soul is here to do' — three angles on the same life.
